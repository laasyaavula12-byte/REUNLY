A COMMUNITY WE CAN TRUST

A little care goes a long way.

REUNLY works best when everyone reports honestly and respects other students' belongings.

  • Provide accurate item descriptions.
  • Never claim an item that isn't yours.
  • Verify ownership before handing over an item.
  • Keep personal information private.
  • Arrange handovers in safe campus locations.
